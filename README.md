Dustline is a round-based tactical FPS in a sun-baked freight depot: 5v5, plant/defuse objective, buy economy, and bots that patrol, hold and react. Built with three.js.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

## Play

Ember (you + 4 bot teammates) attack two sites — the Silo at A and the Crane at B — against 5 Azure defender bots. Plant the spike or eliminate the enemy team to take the round; first to 5 rounds wins the match. Earn money for kills, plants and round wins, and spend it in the buy menu (B) on rifles, SMGs, a sniper and frag grenades.
